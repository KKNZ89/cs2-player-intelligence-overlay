    Hotkeys

    Pin overlay Click the field and press a key combination, for example F8 or Ctrl+Shift+I. Shortcuts used by other apps are refused.

    Data sources

    Leetify needs no key or sign-in. The others are optional and add data. Keys are stored encrypted on this PC; the code next to a saved key is the start of its SHA-256 hash, so you can tell which key is saved without showing it.

    CSRep sign-in optional
    Signed out, CSRep's pages show only the Trust Score and verdict. Signed in, they also show its stats overview: K/D, ADR, HLTV rating, KAST and time to damage. You sign in through Steam on csrep.gg itself; the app never sees a password. Not needed with a CSRep API key.
    CSStats sign-in
    You sign in on csstats.gg itself, in a separate window; the app never sees your password. When you close that window, your own profile is looked up to check the sign-in.
    Source priority when several sources publish a value
      Applies to K/D, win rate, HS %, FACEIT level and Elo, time to damage, rating and ADR. The first source with a value wins; the value's source shows on hover. API sources are first by default; CSStats and CSRep values are read from page text.

      Leetify, Steam profiles and CSRep need no account. Each source is queried at its own pace; source health per player is under Diagnostics.

      Getting started

      1. The first time, go to Settings → General and click Install GSI config. This adds a small file to CS2's cfg folder so the game tells the app what is going on. Restart CS2 after that.
      2. Start the app, then CS2. The app can also start CS2 for you (Settings → General).
      3. Join a match. Players show up on the Match page as the app finds them. Lookups start right away.
      4. In game, hold Tab to see the overlay. Hold Ctrl+Tab for more columns.

      CS2 has to run in borderless or windowed mode. In exclusive fullscreen nothing can be drawn over the game. Use the overlay on a second monitor instead (Settings → Overlay).

      Teams and colours: turn on Read CS2's scoreboard in Settings → Overlay. Hold Tab for a moment in a match and the app puts everyone in your team or the other one (marked "Scoreboard"), and shows each player's CS2 colour as the ring around their avatar. Your own Team/Enemy choice always wins. It's off by default because it captures the CS2 window.

      F8 and the clickable-overlay key can be changed in Settings → Hotkeys. Use your configured clickable-overlay key for mouse navigation: the overlay temporarily takes focus so you can click player cards and profile links, even if CS2 keeps its own cursor captured. Press Esc or the shortcut again to exit, then click back into CS2. Switching to another window also ends mouse navigation. The automatic Esc-menu view and Tab views do not take focus.

      Not assigned means the player's team is not known, not that the player is missing. Steam's recent-player list does not identify teams. Choose Teammate/Opponent on the Match page, or Team/Enemy in an Esc-menu player card. Players you spectate on your own side in Competitive, Premier or Wingman can be confirmed automatically; party-based and inferred assignments are labelled and can be corrected manually.

      Where the numbers come from

      • Leetify: aim, positioning, utility, rating and recent matches. Works without an account.
      • FACEIT: level and Elo. Add your own free FACEIT key for match stats and profile links.
      • CSStats: K/D, ADR, HLTV rating. Off until you turn it on in Settings → Data sources, and needs you to sign in on csstats.gg once.
      • CSRep: trust score and verdict. Add a CSRep API key, or allow reading its public pages in Settings → Data sources. Sign in to CSRep there as well and you also get its K/D, ADR and rating.
      • Steam: hours in CS2 and bans. Needs a Steam Web API key.

      A value shows N/A when the source didn't have it. Hover over it to see why. Private profiles and new accounts are the usual reasons. The Diagnostics page lists every lookup per player.

      Your keys are stored encrypted on this PC. The app never asks for or stores a password. CSStats sign-in happens on their own site.

      If CSRep asks for a security check, the Match page shows Complete CSRep check: complete the check in the window that opens, close it, and every blocked player is looked up again. If a site asks for Cloudflare verification or cookie preferences elsewhere, open its profile from a player card and complete the prompt yourself, then refresh the player. CSRep and CSStats windows share the browser sessions used for lookups. CSRep keeps its automatic "Deny all" choice for optional cookies when available; other consent prompts require your decision. The app never solves security challenges; sites may still restrict embedded browsers.

      Profile flags

      The Profile column compares a player's Premier rating with their FACEIT level, CS2 hours, account age and Leetify aim. If two or more of these don't fit together, the player is marked Review. Hover over it to see which ones.

      This is a hint, not proof. Smurfs, returning players and shared accounts all trip it. If there wasn't enough data to compare, it says so instead of guessing. You can turn the column off in Settings → Analysis.

      Match history and notes

      Every match you finish is saved on this PC: who you played, with or against, and their stats at the time. The History page lists your matches; open one to see its players. Above the list, Your performance by map sums your own kills, deaths, assists and MVPs from each recorded match, with a K/D trend per map. A player's details → Matches also groups their recent Leetify matches by map (shown live, not saved). Open a player on the Match page to see your past games together. Leetify data is never saved.

      Notes: add notes about a player in their details (Notes tab), in the overlay's player card (press Shift+F8 first so you can type), or on the History page. Each note keeps the date, time, match, and whether they were with or against you. When you meet that player again, their notes show in the overlay card and here, newest first. You can export, import or clear the history in Settings → Database.

      Privacy

      The app sends no usage data anywhere. It only contacts the sources above to look up players, and GitHub to check for updates.

      Everything it keeps stays on this PC, in %APPDATA%\nz.local.cs2playerintel: your settings, encrypted API keys, match history and notes, the log, and the browser sessions for CSStats and CSRep. With scoreboard reading on, the last scoreboard picture that showed nothing is kept there too, as scoreboard-last.png. Settings → Database deletes the history and notes. When you uninstall, tick "Delete the application data" to remove that folder too.

      When something is wrong

      • No players: check that Game state says Connected (bottom left). If not, install the GSI config and restart CS2.
      • Some players are missing: CS2 only reports the players it shows you, so some appear later in the match. You can add a SteamID by hand on the Match page.
      • Overlay doesn't show: CS2 is probably in exclusive fullscreen. Switch to borderless.
      • A source shows nothing: open Diagnostics. CSStats and CSRep sometimes ask for a browser check; open the player's CSRep or CSStats page once and the lookups work again.

      Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub and paste the log from Diagnostics → Copy last match (or attach the file from Open folder).
